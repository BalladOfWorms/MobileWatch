## MobileWatch v1.1.1

### Improved

- **Vana'diel clock.** The clock button in the header shows the current
  Vana'diel time and date, the day of the week and moon phase, Japan time, and
  how long until each upcoming day and moon phase.
- **Music from several folders.** Add as many music folders as you like; each
  one adds to the library. The folder button lists them, and any folder can be
  taken out again (nothing on your phone is deleted).
- **Remove tracks from the list.** Select tracks and tap the bin to take them off
  the list. Adding their folder again brings them back.
- **Playlists.** The playlist button at the top of the player switches between
  all tracks and your playlists, and creates, renames and deletes them. Select
  tracks to add them to a playlist.
- **The now-playing bar is blue,** so it stands apart from the gold of the track
  playing in the list, and long titles slide sideways instead of being cut off.
- **Zone music.** Opening a zone in Zones plays a few seconds of that zone's music
  from your library, fading in and out, and the zone page shows which track it is
  with a button to play it in full. Each zone's music comes from the game's own
  zone data, so towns, fields, ships, airships, Residential Areas and the past
  [S] areas all play what they play in game. It never interrupts music that's
  already playing, and can be switched off in Settings.
- **Every track plays,** including the game's newer music (Abyssea onwards),
  which is stored in a different format. Abyssea, the airships and every
  Dynamis area now have their zone music.
- **Quests brought up to date.** 1,116 quests, now matching OmniWatch's checklist:
  the Unlocking a Myth quests file under Aht Urhgan as they do in the game's log,
  Lure of the Wildcat is listed once in each area it appears in, and over twenty
  quests are added across Jeuno, the Outlands and Adoulin. Your ticks carry over.

### Changed

- **More room in the Items, Bestiary and Zones tabs:** a slimmer search box, and
  the server population on a single line.

### Fixed

- **The Divine and The Serpentine Labyrinth** now have the right names.
- **Grav'iton and Revenant Maiden are listed once.** Each appears a single time
  even when both of the game's copies are in your library.

## MobileWatch v1.1.0

### New

- **Music player.** The ♪ button in the header opens a player for FINAL FANTASY
  XI's soundtrack, played from your own game files. Choose the folder you've
  copied the game's music into once, and every track is listed.
  - Tracks **loop the way the game loops them**: the intro once, then the loop
    section seamlessly. The repeat button switches to playing each track once,
    then the next.
  - Tracks are **named automatically**, with composer, expansion and where you
    hear each one in game, for the original game through The Voracious
    Resurgence.
  - MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG and M4A files play too.
  - Favourites, search, shuffle and seeking.
  - **Select tracks** with a long press, or select them all at once, then
    favourite the selection or play it as its own queue.
- **Plays in the background**, with the screen off or another app in front, and
  has **controls in the notification and on the lock screen**. Headset and
  Bluetooth buttons work too.

### Changed

- **Darker look** across the whole app.

No music is included with MobileWatch; the player uses the files from your own
copy of the game.

---

Installs over earlier versions — no need to uninstall. Requires Android 8.0 or
newer. On Android 13 and newer, MobileWatch asks once to show notifications, for
the player controls.

## MobileWatch v1.0.1

Bug fixes only — no new features.

### Fixed

- **Text no longer breaks mid-word on larger display font sizes.** Labels in
  Settings, the hobby guild panels and several info rows were wrapping into
  fragments ("Bestiar / y", "G u i l d") when the system font scale was turned
  up. Rows now size themselves to the text they're given instead of using fixed
  widths, so they hold at any font scale.
- **Mountain Peiste** is now correctly listed as aggressive.

---

Installs over v1.0.0 — no need to uninstall. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
