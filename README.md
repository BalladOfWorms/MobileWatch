## MobileWatch v1.0.0

A native Android companion for **Final Fantasy XI** — bestiary, zones, items,
jobs, crafts and more, in one offline-first app. First public release.

**Requires Android 8.0 or newer · ~61 MB**

### Installing

MobileWatch is not on the Google Play Store, so your phone will treat it as an
unknown app. That is normal for a sideloaded APK and nothing is wrong.

1. Download `MobileWatch-1.0.0.apk` below.
2. Tap it. Android will say your browser isn't allowed to install apps — tap
   **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
3. Tap the file again and choose **Install**.
4. Play Protect will warn about an unrecognised developer. Tap
   **More details → Install anyway**.

### What's inside

- **Bestiary** — 6,810 monsters across 218 families, with 1,591 ability
  descriptions covering element, target shape and effects
- **Zones** — 294 zones: maps, travel, battlefields, quests, NM rosters
- **Items** — 23,503 items with live Auction House pricing by world
- **Jobs** — traits, abilities, spells, 97 Beastmaster jug familiars, 22 avatars
- **Hobbies** — fishing, the nine crafts, gardening, chocobo digging and more
- **Trusts · Chains · Content** — 122 alter egos, 230 weapon skills, Dynamis guides

Everything works offline except Auction House prices and zone maps; maps
download once per zone and are cached on the device afterwards.
